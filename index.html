Every market has its gatekeepers. Miru Partners makes the introduction.

Based in the Netherlands, Miru Partners helps ambitious companies decide where to expand and arrive with partners already expecting them.

Where Miru Partners Steps In

Expansion tends to stall at predictable moments: the decision, the first meeting, the launch plan and the paperwork. Each of our services meets one of those moments head on.

Market Opportunity Assessment

Miru Partners quantifies addressable demand and benchmarks your pricing against established local competitors. We conduct interviews with buyers and industry insiders then test the findings, leading to a go or no-go recommendation for each shortlisted market.

Read More

Partner and Distributor Introductions

Drawing on long-standing relationships across European trade, Miru Partners shortlists distributors and channel partners whose portfolios complement yours. Each introduction comes with context on both sides, so first meetings move straight to commercial terms.

Read More

Entry Strategy and Due Diligence

Each entry model is weighed for its tax and liability implications. Due diligence on prospective partners and local regulation then shapes a phased 90-day roadmap with named owners and milestones.

Dutch Market Set-Up

Miru Partners manages the formalities of establishing a business. A professional website and domain email complete the set-up, so the business is ready to invoice from the moment it arrives.
